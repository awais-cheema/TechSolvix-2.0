Privacy Policy

Effective Date: April 1, 2026

TechSolvix ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect the information you provide when using our website or engaging our services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide through our contact forms, project inquiries, emails, or other communications.

  • Full Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Company Name (if provided)
  • Project Requirements and Messages

We may also automatically collect certain technical information including your IP address, browser type, device information, operating system, and pages visited on our website.

2. How We Use Your Information

The information we collect may be used to:

  • Respond to project inquiries.
  • Prepare quotations and proposals.
  • Communicate regarding ongoing projects.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Send important project-related updates.
  • Comply with legal obligations where applicable.

3. Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience and analyze website performance. We may also use analytics tools to understand visitor behavior and improve our services.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, certain features of the website may not function properly.

4. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party providers to deliver our services, including web hosting providers, domain registrars, payment processors, Google services, Meta services, analytics providers, cloud storage providers, and communication platforms.

These third-party providers maintain their own privacy policies, and we encourage users to review them where applicable.

5. Data Sharing

TechSolvix does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

We may share information only when necessary to:

  • Provide requested services.
  • Complete website deployment or hosting setup.
  • Process payments through authorized providers.
  • Comply with applicable laws or legal requests.

6. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to safeguard your personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. While we strive to protect your information, no online system can guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, or enforce our agreements.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of your personal information where legally permissible.
  • Withdraw consent for future communications.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for businesses and individuals aged 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

10. External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites for your convenience. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of those external websites.

11. Updates to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, legal requirements, or business practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with the revised effective date.
