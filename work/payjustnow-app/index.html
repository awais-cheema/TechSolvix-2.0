PayJustNow

An App Redesign Built for Discovery

2020 - Present
Location
South Africa
Services
  • UI Design

Visit Website

The Brief

Overview

PayJustNow, South Africa’s leading Buy Now, Pay Later (BNPL) platform, lets customers split purchases into three interest-free instalments. As one of our longest-standing clients, we’ve worked closely with their team to evolve the app to meet a growing market. Moving beyond payment management and checkout, our goal was to help shape a platform that could grow into a competitive marketplace and enrich their customers' experience.

Challenge

BNPL in South Africa is a rapidly growing yet relatively uncharted space, which placed PayJustNow in a position to lead the way. The challenge was to evolve their app into a competitive, feature-rich product experience while setting a new standard for local fintech design.

Solution

To build a product that truly stood apart from its competitors, we set out to rival the biggest international brands and apply global best practices within a uniquely South African market. Through multiple iterations, research, testing, and the creation of a scalable design system, we aided in restructuring the app’s overall UI, navigation and content structure to create a richer brand and deal discovery experience.

A Digital Brand Experience

Having worked alongside Parsons Agency during their exceptional rebrand of PayJustNow, one of our key roles was to translate the bold new identity into the app experience. We focused on creating a competitive UI that gave customers a clear window into the brand, while elevating the shopping journey with a multilayered Discover page that integrates featured deals and partner brands, offering a snapshot of everything PayJustNow has to offer.

Crafted for Clarity

A key component of the UI redesign was honouring the new brand at every touchpoint. This meant carefully crafting a consistent icon pack that stayed true to PayJustNow’s identity while remaining highly functional and globally accessible.

Seamless Discovery

Categorisation was a key usability feature we built into the app, enabling us to surface the most relevant features, brands, and deals in a way that made the experience feel personalised and silky smooth to navigate.
